### Wired vs. Wireless: What Actually Changes

Wired connections remove two variables at once — battery state and Bluetooth pairing. Community reports consistently show that players who switch from Bluetooth to a direct USB cable resolve "controller not detected" problems more often than any other single fix.

Wireless is still perfectly viable. Just know that Bluetooth interference, low batteries, and competing devices can all mimic a game bug.

## How to Enable Deified Controller Support Step by Step

Work through these steps in order. Most players never need to go past step four.

| Step | Action | Why It Matters |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Connect the controller **before** launching the game | Many titles scan for input devices only at startup |
| 2 | Open Steam (or your launcher) and confirm the controller appears in settings | Proves the OS sees the hardware |
| 3 | Enable Steam Input for the game, or set it to "Forced Off" if prompts look wrong | Resolves mismatched button icons |
| 4 | Launch the game and open the controls menu | Confirms the game detects the pad |
| 5 | Switch input mode from keyboard to gamepad if the option exists | Some menus require a manual toggle |
| 6 | Test every button in a low-stakes area | Catches dead zones and unmapped actions early |
| 7 | Save a custom profile once it feels right | Prevents losing your layout after an update |

If your controller works in the launcher but not in-game, that usually points to Steam Input overriding the game's native detection. Toggling that single setting fixes it in most cases.

### Checking Your Launcher First

Steam, Epic, GOG Galaxy, and the Xbox app all handle controllers slightly differently. Steam is the most flexible because of Steam Input, which lets you translate almost any gamepad into an Xbox-style layout. If you are new to it, the [official Steam Input documentation](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/features/steam_controller) explains how the translation layer works in plain terms.

For other launchers, add the game as a non-Steam shortcut so Steam Input can still manage your controller. It sounds like a workaround because it is — but it works.

### Do Not Overlook the Cable

A surprising number of "dead" controllers are simply running a charge-only USB cable. Charge-only cables carry power but no data, so the PC never sees the device. Swap to a known data cable before you reinstall anything.

## Deified Controller Support Troubleshooting

When something breaks, match your symptom to the table below. These are the fixes that come up again and again in player experience threads.

| Symptom | Likely Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Controller works in menus but not in gameplay | Steam Input conflict | Toggle Steam Input on/off and relaunch |
| No input at all | Charge-only cable or dead battery | Swap cable, charge fully, try another USB port |
| Buttons register but are wrong | Layout mismatch (PlayStation vs. Xbox prompts) | Remap in game or apply a Steam Input template |
| Input drops mid-fight | Bluetooth interference or low battery | Switch to wired, move the dongle closer |
| Double inputs / phantom presses | Two input sources active at once | Disconnect the unused device |
| Controller disconnects after alt-tab | Launcher focus issue | Reconnect, then re-enter the game window |
| Stick drift | Hardware wear | Adjust dead zone, then consider replacement |

### The Three Fixes That Solve Most Problems

- **Reconnect while the game is running.** Many players fix detection simply by unplugging and replugging mid-session.
- **Restart the launcher, not just the game.** The launcher's controller service sometimes needs a fresh start.
- **Verify your game files.** A corrupted input config is rare but real, and verification is quick.

If you have tried all three and nothing changes, test the controller in another game. That single test tells you whether the problem lives in your hardware or in your setup.

## Remapping: Building a Layout That Actually Fits

Default layouts are designed for the widest possible audience, which means they are rarely ideal for any individual. A short remapping session pays off for the entire playthrough.

| Action Type | Common Default | Often Better As |
|---|---|---|
| Light attack | Face button | Shoulder button (keeps thumb on stick) |
| Dodge / dash | Face button | Bumper or rear paddle |
| Interact | D-pad | Face button |
| Lock-on / target | Stick click | Dedicated bumper |
| Item use | D-pad | Paddle or trigger modifier |

### Priorities for a Better Layout

1. **Keep your thumbs on the sticks.** Any action you use constantly should live on a shoulder button or paddle.
2. **Avoid stick clicks.** They are imprecise and wear out faster than any other component.
3. **Group related actions.** Attack and dodge should be reachable without repositioning your hand.
4. **Leave one button free for menus.** You will use it more than you expect.

Once you find a layout you like, save it as a named profile. Updates occasionally reset bindings, and a saved profile takes seconds to reload.

## FAQ

### Does Deified have full controller support on PC?

In most cases, deified controller support works out of the box with a wired Xbox-style pad, and it works well. Wireless pads and PlayStation-style controllers generally function too, though you may need to enable Steam Input or accept that on-screen prompts show Xbox icons.

### Why is my controller not detected in Deified?

The three most common causes are a charge-only USB cable, a Bluetooth pairing that silently dropped, and a Steam Input setting conflicting with the game's native detection. Test the controller in another title to rule out hardware failure, then work through the troubleshooting table above.

### Can I remap buttons in Deified?

Yes. Remapping is available through the in-game controls menu, and you can go further with Steam Input if the game's own options feel limited. Community reports suggest that moving dodge and attack onto shoulder buttons is the single most impactful change for most players.

### Which controller is best for Deified?

An Xbox Series X|S wireless controller is the safest pick because Windows recognizes it natively with no extra configuration. If you want paddles and adjustable triggers, step up to an Elite-style pad. If you are on a budget, a DualShock 4 works well once it is paired.

Set up your hardware before you launch, spend ten minutes remapping to your own hands, and save that profile. Do that, and deified controller support stops being a technical hurdle and becomes something you never think about again.
